# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include using welcoming and inclusive language, being respectful of differing viewpoints, gracefully accepting constructive criticism, and focusing on what is best for the community.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
